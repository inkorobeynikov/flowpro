FAQ

Before you send the form

What do I get for free, and where does the money start?

Free: I run the check on your pages, tell you what is failing, which of it actually matters for your business and what each fix involves, and send an itemised estimate. Money starts only if you accept that estimate. Nothing is charged for the audit, the fix list or the conversation about scope.

What do you need from me to start?

For the free audit, just the address. For the work: an editor account or repository access, the pages that matter most to your business, and one 30-minute call to agree the wording of the opening paragraphs and the FAQ. Everything else is my side.

Why is there no fixed price?

Because sites differ more than packages allow. A five-page site with clean templates and a 40-page shop with a custom theme are not the same job, and charging both the same means one of you overpays. The estimate is itemised, agreed in writing before anything starts, and never exceeded without your say-so.

Will this get me cited by ChatGPT?

No one can promise that, and I don't. The platforms decide what they cite, and most brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party pages. What I promise is that every fixed page reaches the top verdict in the same free check you can run yourself.
