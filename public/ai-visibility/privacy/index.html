Effective date: 16 September 2026

Operator: Ivan Karabeinikau Digital Engineering, a sole proprietorship registered in the Polish business register (CEIDG), operating as FlowPro, ul. Bokserska 63, 02-690 Warszawa, Poland. NIP: 9512646879.

AI Visibility Checker is a Chrome extension that audits the page you choose. It only runs when you click its icon; it does not read pages in the background. It processes the page's content, metadata, headings and related files from the audited site's origin entirely in your browser. This information is never sent to the operator.

The extension stores a cached audit result for each site in the browser's local extension storage for one hour so the popup can reopen quickly. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you remove the extension.

When you start an audit, the extension may request robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt from the audited site's own origin. It does not send requests to the operator or to unrelated third parties.

This version has no analytics and no accounts. The optional "Join the waitlist" button opens a normal web page on flowpro.dev in a new tab. Anything you enter on that page is covered by the flowpro.dev privacy policy, not by this one.

If we add accounts or paid checks, we will update this policy before release and ask for your consent in the extension.
