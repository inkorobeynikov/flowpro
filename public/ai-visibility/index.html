What it checks

Six signals that help machines understand your site

The extension looks for common technical and content issues that can make a page harder for AI assistants and search engines to access or interpret.

R

AI crawler access

Reads robots.txt rules for crawlers used by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

S

Sitemap.xml

Checks whether a sitemap is available and can help crawlers discover important pages.

L

Llms.txt

Looks for an llms.txt file that gives AI systems a concise guide to your site and content.

{ }

Structured data

Finds JSON-LD and reviews whether the page supplies useful machine-readable context.

M

Meta and Open Graph

Reviews titles, descriptions, canonical details, and social preview tags for completeness.

H

Content structure

Checks headings and opening copy for a clear hierarchy that is easier to interpret.

0–100 score
One score, followed by the fixes that matter most.

Results are grouped and prioritized so you can start with blockers, then work through useful improvements. The score is a diagnostic guide, not a promise of inclusion or ranking.

FAQ

Quick answers

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimization. It is the practice of making your content easy for AI assistants and search engines to access, understand, and reference when answering questions.

Is AI Visibility Checker free?

Yes. The Chrome extension is free to install and use.

What data does the extension collect?

The extension does not collect or transmit the pages you audit. Checks run locally in your browser. If you join the launch waitlist, the email address you submit is handled separately for that purpose.
