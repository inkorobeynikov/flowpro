The problem

Four things that stop AI assistants from reading a site

🚫

Crawlers blocked by accident

A single Disallow written for one bot, or a firewall rule, keeps GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot out. OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity each document this: a blocked search bot means your pages are not in the pool of sources.

📄

Files that lie

An llms.txt or sitemap.xml returns status 200 but serves the home page as HTML. Status codes pass; content fails. Our own site did this before we checked.

🏷

No machine-readable facts

Company name, services, prices and FAQ exist only as design, not as visible text or JSON-LD. Systems that fetch a page read the HTML, not the layout.

✂️

Slogan first, answer last

The opening paragraph says “Small agency. Big-team pace.” and the useful sentence is in section four. Studies of LLM citations find the first third of a page is where most cited text comes from.

What the score is — and isn't

It measures whether your site is accessible and readable for AI systems, using rules from platform documentation and published studies. It does not measure whether AI assistants mention you: most brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party pages, not from the brand's own site. The score tells you your side of the job is done.

FAQ

Questions people ask before installing

Is the extension really free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, makes no account and sends nothing to us. So is the audit I do myself if you send me your address. Only the implementation work is paid, to an estimate you agree first.

What does “Ready to be cited” mean?

It is the top verdict of the checker: crawlers are allowed, files are valid, facts are in visible HTML and JSON-LD, and every page starts with a direct answer. It means the technical preconditions are met. It is not a promise that any assistant will cite the page.

Do I need llms.txt?

For Google Search, no — Google states it ignores the file. No AI platform has confirmed reading it, and a 2026 study of 137 000 sites found 97 % of llms.txt files get no requests. Coding agents do read it. The checker treats it as a small, cheap improvement, not a blocker.

Does structured data get me cited?

Adding JSON-LD did not increase AI citations in the largest controlled study so far (Ahrefs, 1 885 pages, 2026). It still helps search engines disambiguate your business and qualifies pages for rich results. The checker scores it as useful hygiene, weighted below crawler access and content structure.

What if my site is on a custom platform?

Send the URL through the form. I look at what the fixes would actually involve on your stack and say so within one business day. If something is impractical there, I tell you that instead of quoting it.
