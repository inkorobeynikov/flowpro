Why this exists

The checker started as an audit of my own site

flowpro.dev shipped with an llms.txt that returned the home page as HTML, a sitemap nobody had validated, and a first paragraph that was a slogan rather than an answer. I wrote the checks to find that on my own site, then kept going because most small-business sites fail the same list.

That is also why the wording here is careful. Whether an AI assistant cites you depends on the platform and on what other sites say about you. What a site owner controls is narrower: access, valid files, machine-readable facts, and a page that answers the question near the top. That part is checkable, so that is the part I sell.
